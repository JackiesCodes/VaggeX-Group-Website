ABOUT
COMPANY

Vaggex Group is a proudly citizen-owned construction, civil works, and infrastructure solutions company based in Botswana. Established with a vision to rebuild, maintain, and advance the nation's infrastructure, Vaggex Group combines technical experience, business leadership, and a commitment to excellence in delivering world-class construction and maintenance solutions.

Though newly registered, Vaggex Group is built on decades of professional experience through its founding team. Our directors and partners bring proven expertise in civil engineering projects, water transfer schemes, rehabilitation works, public facilities, and project management, alongside strong financial, business, and entrepreneurial skills.

Vaggex Group is not just a contractor – we are a strategic partner for development, offering solutions that improve communities, enhance economic growth, and deliver long-term value.

VISION, MISSION
& CORE VALUES

  • Client-Centricity – Listening, adapting, and delivering tailored solutions.

OUR
SERVICES

At Vaggex Group, we deliver end-to-end construction and maintenance solutions designed to exceed expectations. Our expertise spans residential, commercial, and industrial projects—covering everything from new builds and renovations to long-term facility upkeep

1.Civil & Infrastructure Construction

We specialize in developing durable infrastructure projects that meet growing demands for modernization:

  • Roads, bridges, and drainage systems
  • Pipelines, water reticulation, and wastewater solutions
  • Civil structures and public facilities

2.Building Construction

Delivering high-quality, safe, and functional structures across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors:

  • Housing developments and offices
  • Schools, clinics, and institutional facilities
  • Fit-outs, renovations, and expansions

3.Rehabilitation & Maintenance

Extending the lifespan of infrastructure through professional care and refurbishment:

  • Preventive and corrective maintenance
  • Structural rehabilitation and facility upgrades
  • Plumbing, electrical, and general maintenance services

4.Project Management & Consultancy

Offering trusted expertise to ensure successful project execution:

  • Site supervision and quality control
  • Cost estimation, budgeting, and reporting
  • Design coordination and feasibility studies

OUR
CAPABILITIES

Although Vaggex Group is a new entity, our capability lies in the strength of our leadership team. Collectively, our directors bring over 30 years of industry experience, spanning large-scale water transfer projects, educational institutions, public facilities, rehabilitation works, and infrastructure upgrades.

Experience-Backed Leadership

Mr. Vehonga Kangotui has served as a General Foreman on multi-million-pula projects across Botswana, working with companies such as China State Construction, Sharps Construction, Bulongo Projects, Agnoat Construction and China Harbour Engineering JV. His extensive knowledge of pipelines, pump stations, reservoirs, treatment plants, and building projects forms the backbone of our technical expertise.

Business & Financial Expertise

Mr. Albin Mashabe, a Bachelor of Commerce (Entrepreneurship) graduate, strengthens the group with skills in business development, financial management, and strategic planning. His background ensures the company operates with efficiency, sustainability, and growth in mind.

Compliance & Resources

Vaggex Group is structured to meet CIPA and PPADB requirements, with capacity to scale resources, workforce, and partnerships for projects of varying size and complexity.

This blend of field-tested technical know-how and business leadership uniquely positions Vaggex Group as a competent, reliable, and growth-oriented construction partner.

LEADERSHIP
TEAM

Director
